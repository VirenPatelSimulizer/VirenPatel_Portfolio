Decoded

How breakthrough ideas become real industries

Sample → Sale → Scale
Spotify Apple Podcasts

"Decoded is the story of the people who build the things we depend on — and the work no one ever sees them do. Every product makes a journey almost nobody talks about: from a sample that barely works, to a sale that proves someone wants it, to the brutal scale-up that turns it into an industry. Each stretch demands a completely different kind of brilliance. Some episodes are conversations with founders, engineers, and operators living in that gap; others are just me, working through something I've seen up close."
