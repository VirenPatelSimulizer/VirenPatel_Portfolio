How it works: Neutral particles travel with random directions. Donors have an extra electron to give. Acceptors need one electron for a full octet. When a Donor collides with an Acceptor, they exchange an electron — the Donor becomes a Cation (+) and the Acceptor becomes an Anion (−). Coulomb forces then govern all charged particles.
