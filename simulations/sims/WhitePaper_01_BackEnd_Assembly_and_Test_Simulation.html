Disclaimer

This simulation models semiconductor manufacturing variation and reliability mechanisms using publicly available research and textbooks.

All modeled mechanisms (wire bond defects, microbump failures, delamination, gate oxide breakdown, electromigration, etc.) are derived from open academic and industry literature.

References include:

  • Semiconductor Packaging — A. Chen
  • Semiconductor Advanced Packaging — J. H. Lau
  • Reliability Physics and Engineering — J. W. McPherson
  • IEEE / Microelectronics Reliability publications
  • Public semiconductor reliability handbooks

This project does not use any proprietary manufacturing data, internal process details, or confidential information from any semiconductor company. All failure mechanisms are modeled at a high-level educational abstraction for demonstration purposes only.
